## TTRPG callouts

> [!professor] Professor Phineas Phantomhive II
> *"A mirror does not lie. It only forgets which side of the glass you were standing on."*

> [!read-aloud] Read aloud
> The fire has burned low. Somewhere above you, a glass cracks, though no one in the room has moved.

> [!handout] A letter, sealed with silver wax
> *To whoever finds this:* the inn does not stand where it stood last winter. Follow the lantern, not the road.

> [!rumor] Heard in the taproom
> They say the innkeeper never sleeps, and that the mirror behind the bar shows the room as it was a hundred years ago.

> [!loot] Treasure in the vault
> - 120 gp
> - Silvered dagger
> - Potion of Healing ×2
> - A cracked hand mirror

> [!lore] The Mirrorgate
> Long before the realms had names, the gods fought a war whose scars still cut through the world. Where the glass broke deepest, the Mirrorgate opened.

> [!trap] Glass floor
> **DC 15 Perception** to notice the hairline cracks. A creature that steps on it falls 20 ft. into the vault below.

> [!rule] Scar hazard
> **Trigger:** a creature ends its turn in a scar. **Effect:** DC 13 Wisdom save or it sees a reflection that is not its own.

> [!music] Ambience
> Low strings, a slow harp, and wind against glass. Loop quietly under the scene.

> [!timeline] The Eternal Lady
> - **Year 0** The inn appears in the Forest of Thieves.
> - **Year 40** Maria Rose sends her letter.
> - **Now** The luminous plants reach the old road.

> [!location] To the Eternal Lady
> A warm inn in a sheltered hollow, two miles off the old road, reached by an unmarked path.

> [!quest] Find Maria Rose
> - [ ] Reach the Temple of the Moon in Water
> - [ ] Follow the luminous plants
> - [ ] Learn what Maria found

> [!secret] What the archivist hides
> The reflection in the lens has been running the archive for years.

> [!shattered] The broken mirror
> Cracks spread from the corner where something struck the glass. Also works as `[!mirror]`.

> [!prophecy] The old warning
> When the moon drinks the river and the glass forgets its maker, seven will sit at one table.

> [!curse] The innkeeper's bargain
> Whoever breaks the seals beneath the altar will never again see their own reflection.

> [!vision] What the lens showed
> A forest full of light, and a door standing open with no wall around it. Also works as `[!dream]`.

> [!combat] Ambush on the old road
> - **4 bandits**, **1 bandit captain**
> - Half cover behind the fallen cart
> - The captain flees at half HP

> [!clue] Found under the bar
> A receipt for 40 silver mirrors, signed with Maria Rose's initials, dated next spring.

> [!divine] Blessing of the Moon
> Each creature that prays at the altar gains 1d4 temporary hit points and Advantage on its next Wisdom save. Also works as `[!holy]`.

> [!arcane] Ward-sigil
> A circle of silver runes under the floorboards. *Detect Magic* reveals strong abjuration. Also works as `[!magic]`.

> [!weather] Storm over the forest
> Heavy rain: Disadvantage on Perception checks that rely on hearing; open flames go out.
